# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**

Kepler respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights available to you.

---

## 1. Who Controls Your Data?

Kepler is responsible for determining how personal information is collected and used within the Kepler application and its services.

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, you can contact us through the support options provided in the application.

The current support email is **keplerstudy@gmail.com**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide and improve Kepler.

Depending on how you use the application, this may include your name, email address, account information, selected university, selected modules and other information you choose to provide.

We may also collect information about your use of Kepler, such as study activity, completed topics, progress information, preferences and interactions with application features.

If you upload notes, lecture slides or other study materials, we collect that content along with information about when it was uploaded and, where relevant, which module or topic it relates to.

If you voluntarily submit feedback, complaints, suggestions or support requests, we may collect the information contained in those submissions.

We may also receive technical information associated with your device or application session, such as device-related information, application version, operating-system information and diagnostic information.

If you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect information about your subscription status through our payment provider. We do not collect or store your card details — those are handled entirely by Google Play.

---

## 3. Information You Provide

You may provide information when creating an account, selecting your institution or modules, using study features, uploading notes or study materials, submitting feedback, contacting support or using other Kepler functionality.

You should only provide information that is reasonably necessary for the feature you are using.

Do not submit passwords, financial information, highly sensitive personal information or another person's private information through free-text fields or uploaded material unless the application specifically requires it.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to create and manage your account.

We may use your information to provide personalised study experiences, including displaying your selected university, modules, study content and progress.

We may use information to maintain and improve application functionality, troubleshoot technical problems and develop new features.

We may use information to communicate with you about your account, important service notices, support requests and other necessary service-related matters.

We may use information to manage your subscription, verify whether it is active, and process free trials or renewals through the app store.

We may use aggregated or de-identified information to understand how Kepler is used and improve the service.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing, maintaining, securing or improving Kepler unless permitted by law or otherwise explained to you.

---

## 5. Study and Progress Data

Kepler may store information about your academic activity within the application, such as selected modules, topics studied, completed activities, study sessions and progress.

This information allows Kepler to provide features such as progress tracking, personalised study experiences and study recommendations.

Study activity, including mastery percentages and progress indicators, reflects your activity within the app. It is not an official university academic record and is not a prediction or guarantee of any exam result or grade.

---

## 6. Notes and Materials You Upload

If Kepler's upload features are available to you, any notes, summaries, lecture slides or other material you upload ("User Content") is stored by Kepler and is private to your account.

Your uploaded material and any AI-generated notes, quizzes, flashcards and recall sessions built from it are visible only to you. They are not shown to other students using the same module.

You are responsible for making sure you have the right to upload any material you submit. Do not upload lecture slides, textbook material, past papers or other content that you do not personally own or have permission to use.

You can delete any of your uploaded material or generated notes at any time through the application, and the associated content will be removed from our active systems.

---

## 7. AI and Your Information

Kepler relies on artificial intelligence to provide its core study features. When you upload study material or ask a question, relevant content is sent to third-party AI providers so that they can generate a response.

Kepler currently uses the following AI providers: **DeepSeek, Google Gemini, and Figviz**.

- **DeepSeek** generates study notes, practice questions, quizzes and flashcards from your uploaded material.
- **Google Gemini** powers the Ask Tutor feature and grades Active Recall answers.
- **Figviz** generates labelled diagrams for certain concepts where a visual aids understanding.

Content sent to these providers is used only to generate a response for you. These providers process the data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes or to retain it beyond what is needed to produce the response.

You should avoid submitting unnecessary personal, confidential or highly sensitive information into AI features.

AI-generated responses may be incorrect and should not be treated as an official academic record, authoritative source, or guarantee of any exam outcome.

## 8. Cookies, Analytics and Similar Technologies

Kepler and its service providers may use technical identifiers, analytics or similar technologies where necessary to operate, secure and improve the application.

These technologies may help us understand application performance, diagnose errors and understand general usage patterns.

Where legally required, we will provide appropriate choices or obtain consent for technologies that require consent.

## 9. Where Your Data Is Stored

Kepler uses cloud-based infrastructure to store and process information required to operate the application.

Depending on the service being used, information may be stored or processed on servers located outside your country of residence.

Kepler uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication, cloud database, cloud functions, file storage, push notifications, app integrity checks, crash reporting and analytics.

Kepler also uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, and Google Play Billing to process payments. Payment card details are never stored by Kepler.

The exact location of individual data may depend on the configuration and infrastructure of the relevant service.

---

## 10. Who May Receive Your Information?

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate Kepler, including:

- **Google** (Firebase authentication, database, storage, functions, messaging, analytics, crash reporting, app check, and Google Play Billing).
- **RevenueCat** (subscription management).
- **DeepSeek**, **Google Gemini**, and **Figviz** (AI processing for notes, tutor, recall grading and diagrams).

These providers may only receive information necessary to perform their services for Kepler or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Information may also be disclosed where required to comply with applicable law, legal processes, security requirements, or to protect the rights and safety of Kepler, its users or others.

## 11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Security measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, encrypted connections and security features provided by our infrastructure providers.

All data sent between the app and our servers, and between our servers and third-party AI providers, is encrypted in transit.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong password where applicable and avoid sharing your account credentials.

---

## 12. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain personal information, including uploaded study materials, only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Kepler, maintain your account, fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymise it or securely dispose of it where reasonably possible.

Some information may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion before those backups are overwritten or securely deleted.

The exact retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

---

## 13. Account Deletion

You may delete your Kepler account and all associated personal information, including material you have uploaded, at any time from within the application by going to **Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account**.

You may also request deletion by emailing **keplerstudy@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your account. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

We may need to retain certain information where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements or protect legitimate security interests.

Deletion may not immediately remove information contained in temporary backups or records that must legally be retained.

## 23. Subscriptions and Payments

Kepler offers an optional paid subscription that unlocks premium features. Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Google Play on Android, and through the Apple App Store on iOS where available.

New subscribers may be eligible for a free trial period. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription will automatically renew at the price shown at the time of purchase.

You can cancel your subscription at any time through the subscriptions section of your Google Play or App Store account. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not refund the current period unless required by law.

Kepler does not collect, store or have access to your payment card details. All payment processing is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Kepler receives from RevenueCat and Google Play only the information needed to know whether your subscription is active, in a trial, or expired. This is used solely to unlock the correct features in the app.
